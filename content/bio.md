I am a postdoctoral research associate in the Department of Computational Mathematics, Science and Engineering at Michigan State University, working with [Prof. Andrew J. Christlieb](https://engineering.msu.edu/directory/faculty/christli).

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Missouri University of Science and Technology in 2024, advised by [Prof. Yanzhi Zhang](https://web.mst.edu/zhangyanz/).
My research focuses on numerical methods for kinetic equations, shallow-water moment models, scientific machine learning, and nonlocal and fractional partial differential equations.
